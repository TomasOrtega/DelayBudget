# Code of conduct

DelayBudget is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for
contributors of every background and level of experience.

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, and avoid
personal attacks, intimidation, discrimination, or publication of another
person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate material and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Reports should be made privately to a maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers
will handle reports as confidentially and promptly as practical.
